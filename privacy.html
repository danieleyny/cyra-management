Privacy

Last updated September 16, 2026

This notice describes how information submitted through this website is handled. It is intended as a clear website summary and should be reviewed by legal counsel before final publication.

Information you provide

When you send an inquiry, we receive the details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, inquiry type, property or address, and message.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to respond to your inquiry, coordinate resident or property-related support, and maintain appropriate business records. We do not ask for payment details, government identifiers, or other highly sensitive information through this form.

Form processing

The contact form is processed by Formspree. Information submitted through it is subject to Formspree’s own privacy practices as well as Cyra’s handling of the resulting inquiry.

Retention and sharing

Inquiry details may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide requested service, maintain records, or meet legal obligations. Information may be shared with service providers or property-related parties only when reasonably necessary to address the inquiry or operate the website.

Your choices

You may choose to contact Cyra directly instead of using the online form. To ask about information submitted through this site, email eynymgmt@gmail.com.
